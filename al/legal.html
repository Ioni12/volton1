Kushtet e Përgjithshme të Furnizimit

These Kushtet e Përgjithshme define the terms for the sale, delivery and acceptance of electricity at the metering points specified in the Electricity Marrëveshja e Furnizimit, and set out the rights and obligations of Volton DOOEL Skopje as Supplier and of the Consumer. They form an integral part of the Marrëveshja e Furnizimit; where the Agreement and the Kushtet e Përgjithshme differ, the Agreement prevails. Matters not covered here are governed by the Energy Law, the Supply Rules, the Law on Obligations and other applicable regulations. These Kushtet e Përgjithshme are published on the Supplier's website.

  • Billing Period — the period to which billing of consumption and capacity relates.
  • Consumer — any entity using the purchased electricity for its own consumption.
  • Relevant System Operator — the TSO or DSO delivering electricity to the metering point.
  • Electricity Supply — the sale of electricity to the Consumer.
  • Supplier — any licensed entity authorised to supply and trade electricity.
  • Marrëveshja e Furnizimit — the agreement under which the Supplier delivers contracted electricity and the Consumer pays based on system-operator measurements.
  • Metering Point — any point where electricity is delivered to the Consumer.

The Marrëveshja e Furnizimit is concluded in writing. Metering points are listed in an Annex to the Agreement; the Consumer must promptly report any addition or removal, and is responsible for the accuracy of documentation submitted for registration or supplier changes. Supplied quantities are measured by devices owned by the relevant System Operator, and billing is based on that measurement data.

The Supplier must deliver electricity to each metering point, take on balancing responsibility for the Consumer, and comply with the Energy Law and Supply Rules — including non-discriminatory treatment, clear invoicing, multiple payment and invoice delivery options, transparent and cost-free supplier switching, timely final invoices after a supplier change, and efficient handling of Consumer requests and complaints.

The Consumer must pay invoices on time, keep contact and registration details up to date, notify the Supplier of any decision to renew, terminate or switch supply, avoid interfering with or damaging metering equipment, and allow authorised personnel access. The Consumer is responsible for damage or theft affecting metering equipment where its own fault is established.

Notifications may be delivered by hand, registered mail, e-mail or through the website. If the Kushtet e Përgjithshme or Marrëveshja e Furnizimit are amended, existing Consumers are notified and offered a new agreement with at least 30 days' notice; amendments are published on the website within three days.

Neither party is liable for failure to perform obligations caused by events beyond its control that could not have been foreseen or avoided — including natural disasters, fire, war, civil unrest, government action, network outages, strikes, or a declared energy crisis. The affected party must notify the other promptly and take reasonable steps to limit the impact.

Consumers may complain about invoice calculations, non-performance, denial of the right to switch supplier, or service quality within set deadlines (8 days for an invoice, 15–30 days for other matters). The Supplier must resolve complaints within 30 days and publish its complaint-handling rules.

The Supplier collects and processes personal data necessary to perform the Agreement, applying technical and organisational measures required by the Mbrojtja e të Dhënave Personale Law. Consumers may request information on the data held about them at any time. Confidential information may be shared only where required by law, authorised personnel, or with prior written consent.
