Volton Albania — Partneri juaj i besuar energjetik

Volton Albania (Volton SHPK) is the official sister company of Volton operating in the Republic of Albania. Established with the same commitment to reliable, transparent and sustainable electricity supply, Volton SHPK is a licensed electricity supplier and trader serving business clients connected to medium and high-voltage networks.

Through the same standards of transparency, professionalism and client service that define the Volton group, Volton Albania is committed to contributing to the development of the liberalised energy market and supporting clients with tailor-made energy solutions.
